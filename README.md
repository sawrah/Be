# Be.

An iOS AR breathing and mindfulness app.

Be. uses augmented reality and ambient motion to make breathing exercises 
feel calm and alive. A flower grows and falls with your breath, and a small 
garden grows over time as you practice.

## Features

- **AR breathing** — a flower that responds to your breath, rendered in 
  RealityKit
- **Breath detection** — real-time audio analysis (autocorrelation / vDSP) 
  to follow your actual breathing
- **Hex garden** — an isometric, tap-to-plant garden with seasonal color 
  systems, drawn with SwiftUI Path/Canvas
- **Ambient motion** — splash and orb animations designed to feel like breath

## Built with

SwiftUI · RealityKit · vDSP · Figma
